How Much Does an SIA Badge Cost — and How Do You Get One? (2026)

Updated for the April 2026 fee increase. Covers all frontline SIA badges: Door Supervisor, Security Guard, CCTV and Close Protection.

October 2026 · 6 min read · All Articles

What Is an SIA Badge?

The SIA badge — officially called an SIA licence — is the photocard issued by the Security Industry Authority, the government regulator for the private security industry in the UK. It is a legal requirement under the Private Security Industry Act 2001 for anyone working in a licensable security role.

"Badge" is the term most security professionals use in everyday speech. The SIA uses "licence." They are the same thing — the same card you must carry and display while working.

There are six types of front-line SIA badge. Which one you need depends on the work you will be doing. See SIA Licence Types Explained for a full breakdown of which badge covers which role.

How Much Does an SIA Badge Cost in 2026?

Fee update — April 2026: The SIA licence fee increased to £204 on 1 April 2026. Many guides still show the old fee of £190. Always verify at GOV.UK — Apply for an SIA licence.

The £204 fee covers only the badge application. Training is a separate cost paid directly to your training provider — it is not paid to the SIA. The total amount you spend depends on which badge you are getting and which provider you train with.

The 50% discount (£102 for a second badge) applies when you already hold one SIA licence and apply for a second type.

You pay the £204 fee directly to the SIA — not to your training provider. Any provider asking you to pay the licence fee to them is incorrect. You pay it online when you submit your application at GOV.UK.

How to Get an SIA Badge — Step by Step

The same six steps apply regardless of which badge type you are applying for. The only difference is the training qualification required in step 3.

1

Check you are eligible

You must be 18 or over, have the right to work in the UK, and meet the SIA's criminality criteria. Some convictions can affect your application. The SIA publishes its criteria on GOV.UK.

2

Choose your badge type

Pick the badge that matches the work you want to do. If you are unsure, SIA Licence Types Explained sets out what each badge covers and which roles require it. Most people start with the Door Supervisor badge for its versatility.

3

Complete licence-linked training with an approved provider

You must train with an SIA-approved provider. Check the SIA guidance on licence-linked training for the list of approved awarding organisations. Training providers are approved by those organisations — not directly by the SIA. Do not book with any provider whose awarding organisation is not on the SIA's endorsed list.

4

Apply online at GOV.UK and pay the £204 fee

Once you have your training certificate, apply at GOV.UK — Apply for an SIA licence. You will need proof of identity, proof of right to work, your training certificate, and address history for the last few years. Pay the £204 application fee — it is non-refundable.

5

Complete any identity document checks

The SIA may ask for extra identity checks, or overseas criminal record certificates if you have lived outside the UK.

6

Wait for your badge to arrive

Processing time varies. Use the SIA decision timescale indicator to get an estimate for your specific circumstances. You cannot work in a licensable security role until your badge arrives — do not let any employer tell you otherwise.

Can Someone Else Pay for Your SIA Badge?

Yes. The SIA explicitly supports employer-paid applications — the employer pays the £204 directly to the SIA on your behalf. Many larger security companies will fund both the training and the licence fee for new recruits in exchange for a minimum period of employment. It is worth asking before paying yourself.

Some back-to-work schemes and training providers also offer funding routes for eligible candidates.

How Long Is an SIA Badge Valid?

All frontline SIA badges are valid for three years. They do not renew automatically — you must apply for renewal before your badge expires. If your badge lapses, you cannot work in a licensable role and cannot renew: you must apply as a new applicant. For the full renewal process including the refresher training requirement, see SIA Licence Renewal 2026.
