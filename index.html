Security Jobs UK — Everything You Need to Know

Everything you need to know about UK Security Jobs, SIA licence verification, BS7858 vetting and how our verified security jobs platform works.

UK Security Jobs is the UK's only verified security jobs platform built exclusively for the security industry. Unlike generic job boards, every candidate on UKSecurityJobs is SIA licensed and BS7858 ready — their profile is complete and vetting-ready before they can apply for any role. Security companies get access to qualified, easy-to-vet candidates. Security professionals get access to verified employers and exclusive member benefits.
UK Security Jobs lists vacancies across all SIA licence types including Door Supervisor jobs, Security Guard jobs, CCTV Operator jobs, Close Protection jobs, Cash and Valuables in Transit jobs and Key Holding roles. All security vacancies are posted by verified security companies and are only visible to SIA licensed candidates.
When you register on UK Security Jobs, your SIA licence number is verified in real time against the SIA public register. If your licence is not valid, active or has expired, you cannot access the platform. This means every candidate on UK Security Jobs is confirmed as a currently licensed security professional — giving employers complete confidence before they even see a CV.
BS7858 is the British Standard for the screening of individuals working in a secure environment. It requires a full 5-year employment and address history with no unexplained gaps, right to work verification, and character references. Every candidate profile on UK Security Jobs is structured around BS7858 requirements, making the vetting process significantly faster for security companies.
The Vettability Score is a live score out of 100 that reflects how complete and BS7858-ready a candidate's profile is. It covers SIA licence verification, employment history, address history, right to work documents, referees and more. Candidates scoring below 60 cannot apply for roles. Those scoring 80 and above unlock premium security vacancies. The score updates in real time as candidates complete their profile.
Registration is free for security professionals. Create your account, verify your SIA licence, and build your profile step by step. Once your Vettability Score reaches 60, your profile goes live and you can apply for security vacancies. The full profile includes your employment history, address history, qualifications, right to work documents and referees — everything a security company needs to start vetting immediately.
Security companies register and are verified before gaining access to the candidate pool. Once verified, you can post security vacancies and set a minimum Vettability Score for applicants. Every candidate who applies has already been SIA verified and professionally assessed — meaning you only speak to candidates who are genuinely qualified, vettable and serious about the role.
Verified members of UK Security Jobs get access to exclusive benefits including breakdown cover at preferential rates, mobile phone and SIM deals, PPE and uniform discounts, legal cover for employment rights, gym and fitness discounts, mental health support, CPD and SIA training guidance and career mentoring from experienced industry professionals.
