What’s available

Types of security work in Birmingham

Static security

Static officers work fixed posts at offices, industrial units, retail centres and car parks across Birmingham and the West Midlands. Hours are typically 12-hour shifts, with a mix of days and nights depending on the contract.

Night shifts

Night-shift security is in steady demand across the region, particularly at warehousing and logistics sites around the M6 and M42, and at city centre venues. Night rates are generally higher than day rates.

Weekend work

Weekend work is common in the night-time economy and at weekend events at the NEC, and suits officers fitting security around other commitments.

Part-time security

Part-time and zero-hours roles exist across retail, hospitality and corporate sectors in Birmingham. They can be a way into the industry or a way to build hours flexibly.

Events and venues

The NEC and city-centre venues generate regular event security work. Contracts can be casual or ongoing depending on the operator. A Door Supervisor licence is often required.

Locations

Where the work is

Security work in Birmingham is concentrated in a few key areas. The Bullring and Grand Central are major sources of retail security and CCTV roles. The Mailbox and Paradise district carry demand for corporate access control and static guarding. The NEC in Solihull is one of the largest event venues in Europe and employs large numbers of security officers throughout the year.

The city centre night-time economy creates consistent demand for Door Supervisor-licensed officers. Hospitals across the region employ static security and CCTV operators. Distribution centres along the M6 and M42 corridors generate year-round demand for site security. Industrial estates and construction sites across the wider West Midlands also recruit regularly.

Security pay rates in Birmingham

Based on recent Birmingham job adverts, entry-level venue and retail roles start at around £12.60–£12.95 an hour. Shopping centre night shifts are around £13.80. Experienced static, mobile and door supervisor work typically pays £14–£16+.
