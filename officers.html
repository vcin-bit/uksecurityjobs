A BS7858-ready profile means you have provided everything an employer needs to begin the BS7858 vetting process — a verified 5-year employment history with no unexplained gaps, a complete 5-year address history, reference contacts for every employer, and identity documents. UKSecurityJobs guides you through building this step by step before you apply for any role. Read our full guide to BS7858 vetting →
