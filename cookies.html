1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you signed in and understand how you use the site.

This policy covers cookies used on uksecurityjobs.co.uk and app.uksecurityjobs.co.uk.

3. How to Control Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are set and delete them individually
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (note: this will prevent sign-in from working)

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
