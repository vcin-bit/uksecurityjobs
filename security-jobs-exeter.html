BS7858 is the British Standard for screening individuals in security environments. It requires a fully verified 5-year employment history with no unexplained gaps, a complete 5-year address history, identity verification and criminal record disclosure. Most security employers in Exeter require candidates to pass BS7858 vetting before starting work.
