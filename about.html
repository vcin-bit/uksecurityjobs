About UKSecurityJobs

Built by Someone Who Has Lived This Industry

UKSecurityJobs was not built in a tech incubator by people who have never worked a door or managed a security contract. It was built by someone who has — and who got tired of watching a professional industry being let down by a broken recruitment process.

The Story

Thirty Years. Every Level. One Platform.

The founder of UKSecurityJobs began his career in the British military before entering the UK security industry in 1995 — starting on the ground floor with Pinkertons Security and progressing through every operational and management level to Director positions at national and international security companies. He has also founded and operated his own security businesses.

That career spans the introduction of SIA licensing, the rise of the internet job board, the decline of industry-specific recruitment platforms, and thirty years of watching the same avoidable problem repeat itself: good employers wasting time on unsuitable candidates, and good officers waiting too long to be placed in the right role.

The industry changed. The recruitment process largely did not.

Generic job boards — Indeed, Reed, Totaljobs — were never built for the licensed security sector. They have no mechanism to verify an SIA licence, no understanding of BS7858 vetting requirements, no way to filter by licence type, and no interest in the professional standards the industry is supposed to uphold. The result is hundreds of unsuitable applications for every genuine vacancy, and a vetting process that starts from scratch every single time.

UKSecurityJobs exists to fix that.

"The SIA introduced licensing but missed the opportunity to introduce a complete professional identity for every officer. We built that instead."
Founder — UKSecurityJobs

How We Are Different

What UKSecurityJobs Actually Does

SIA Licence Verified

Every candidate's SIA licence is checked against the public register before their profile goes live. No licence, no access. Simple.

BS7858 Ready Before They Apply

Candidates complete a full BS7858-ready profile — 5-year address history, full employment history, reference contacts — before they can apply for a single role.

No Time Wasters

Incomplete profiles cannot apply. Candidates who accept an interview and do not attend without good reason are banned — three strikes and they are off the platform permanently.

Interview Preparation

Candidates are prepared before every interview — document checklist, scenario-based written assessment, and a video screening to confirm communication skills. Employers know what they are getting before they pick up the phone.

Post-Placement Follow-Up

We check in on every placement one month after they start. Feedback from both candidate and employer. We step in if something is not right. This is not a transaction — it is a professional relationship.

Member Benefits

Verified candidates gain access to exclusive discounts and benefits from partner brands — a genuine investment in the professionals who maintain the standards this industry is supposed to uphold.

Platform Security

How We Protect Your Data

UKSecurityJobs handles some of the most sensitive personal data in the employment sector — National Insurance numbers, SIA licence details, criminal disclosures and full address histories. We take that responsibility seriously.

Data is stored in the European Union in compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Sensitive fields are encrypted at the field level — meaning even within our own infrastructure, the raw values of NI numbers and criminal disclosures are never stored in plain text.

Authentication is handled by an industry-leading provider with multi-factor authentication, session management and bot protection built in. Passwords are never stored by us.

The platform sits behind enterprise-grade security infrastructure including DDoS protection, a web application firewall and bot management. All data in transit is encrypted.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and are actively pursuing Cyber Essentials certification. A formal penetration test will be commissioned prior to full public launch.

For full details of how we collect, store and use your data, see our Privacy Policy.
